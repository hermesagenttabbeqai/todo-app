# User Guide — todo-app

## Getting started
Open the app at: https://hermesagenttabbeqai.github.io/todo-app/

Your tasks are saved automatically in your browser. They stay there when you close and reopen the page. Each person's tasks are private to their own device and browser.

---

## Adding a task
1. Type your task in the **"What needs to be done?"** box at the bottom.
2. Optionally pick a **due date** using the date picker.
3. Tap **Add**.

The task appears in the list immediately.

---

## Completing a task
Tap the **checkbox** on the left of a task to mark it done. The title gets a strikethrough. Tap again to mark it undone.

---

## Editing a task
Tap **Edit** on any task. A dialog opens where you can change the title and/or due date. Tap **Save** to apply, or **Cancel** to discard.

---

## Deleting a task
Tap **Delete** on any task. A confirmation prompt appears — confirm to remove the task permanently.

---

## Due dates and overdue tasks
- Tasks with a due date show the date below the title.
- If a task is past its due date and not yet done, it is highlighted in **red** with an ⚠ Overdue label.
- Tasks are always sorted by due date (earliest first). Tasks without a due date appear last.

---

## Filtering tasks
Use the **All / Active / Completed** buttons at the top to filter the list:
- **All** — shows every task
- **Active** — shows only tasks not yet done
- **Completed** — shows only finished tasks

---

## Task count
The number next to "My Tasks" at the top shows how many tasks are still active (not done).

---

## Data and privacy
All your tasks are stored only in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to any server. Clearing your browser data will erase your tasks.
